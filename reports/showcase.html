Experiment 001 / Measured, not mocked

Small model.
Visible change.

A 135M-parameter language model learns twelve fictional product policies. The base stays frozen. A small LoRA adapter changes the answers.

Baseline phrase pass0%
LoRA phrase pass100%
Trainable parameters3.50%
Training time9.8 min

24 held-out paraphrases of 12 trained facts · Same prompts and greedy decoding · Phrase matches are a heuristic, not semantic accuracy.

How it works

Learn the update.

W + (α / r) BA

The original weights W are frozen. Only low-rank matrices A and B learn from assistant-answer tokens. The best checkpoint is selected by validation loss.

What this does not prove

Recall is only one piece.

Unknown-policy abstention phrase pass: 0% before → 100% after, on only four prompts. Review the actual text: a matching phrase does not guarantee a truthful answer.

The dataset is synthetic and repetitive. Facts overlap across splits; only wording is held out. General reasoning, safety, and capability retention are not established. For changing knowledge, retrieval is often the better tool.
